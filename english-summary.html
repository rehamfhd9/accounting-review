Reading Passage: Workplace Practice

Every organization needs good governance. Governance helps an organization work in an organized, responsible, and honest way. It explains who makes decisions and who is responsible for different tasks. Internal audit examines work, controls, documents, and risks. Its purpose is not only to find mistakes; it also helps the organization improve. Good governance includes responsibility, accountability, transparency, and compliance. In practice, employees should follow approved policies and procedures, keep accurate records, communicate clearly, and ask for guidance when they are uncertain. Managers and auditors should review information fairly, protect confidential documents, and record important decisions. These actions help the organization achieve its objectives and improve its work.

القطعة الثانية — الوحدة 2 — ص15 وص17–18 · 13 عبارة

Reading Passage: Workplace Practice

A clear organizational structure shows departments, positions, reporting lines, and responsibilities. The board provides direction, senior management manages daily activities, and managers plan and control departmental work. Employees should understand their roles, communicate with supervisors, and cooperate with other departments. Delegation gives a task or authority to another person, while the manager remains responsible for checking the work. In practice, employees should follow approved policies and procedures, keep accurate records, communicate clearly, and ask for guidance when they are uncertain. Managers and auditors should review information fairly, protect confidential documents, and record important decisions. These actions help the organization achieve its objectives and improve its work.
